### Dhruva Chakravarthi

I work on getting machines to understand how people move, and how they interact: where two people touch, how their limbs wrap, how far apart they really are, measured in 3D and over time from ordinary video. Independent researcher, based in Bangalore. More at **[dhruva.life](https://www.dhruva.life)**.
